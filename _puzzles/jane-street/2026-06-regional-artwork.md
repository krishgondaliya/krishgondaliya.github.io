The puzzle is built around solving 14 clues whose answers follow a common structural pattern. Each answer begins with the letter A and ends with the letter I, with answer lengths ranging from 2 to 15. The challenge is not simply to solve the individual clues, but to identify how their answers interact with the numerical information provided in the puzzle to reveal a final hidden message.

My approach began by solving the clues independently and examining the relationships between their answers. Once I recognized the shared first and last letters and the distinct answer lengths, I sorted all 14 answers in ascending order by length. This produced a natural ordering from 2 to 15 letters, suggesting that the answer lengths were intended to determine the arrangement rather than serve as incidental properties.

The key breakthrough came from comparing each answer's original clue number with its new position in the sorted list. I calculated the absolute difference between these two positions and used that value as a letter index within the corresponding answer. This extracted one letter from each of the 14 answers. I then rearranged the extracted letters using the sequence provided at the bottom of the puzzle: 7, 8, 10, 15, 12, 9, 13, 6, 11, 3, 14, 4, 2, 5.

The resulting message spelled GEORGE LUCAS HIT, pointing toward a successful film associated with George Lucas. Although *Star Wars* was an obvious initial possibility, the intended answer was AMERICAN GRAFFITI.
